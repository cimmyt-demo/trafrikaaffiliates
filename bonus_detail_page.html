Frequently Asked Questions
What is a welcome bonus wagering requirement?
A wagering (rollover) requirement means you must bet the bonus amount a set number of times before withdrawing. For example, a KES 1,000 bonus with 3x rollover means you must place KES 3,000 in total bets before cashing out winnings.
Which bonus has the lowest wagering requirement in Kenya?
SportPesa's KES 2,000 Free Bet has effectively 1x wagering — the easiest to clear. Betway's KES 5,000 bonus has a 3x requirement, which is the lowest among deposit match bonuses.
Can I claim multiple welcome bonuses?
Welcome bonuses are typically one-per-player per sportsbook. However, you can claim one welcome bonus from each different sportsbook — giving you access to multiple bonuses across different platforms.
Are betting bonuses legal in Kenya?
Yes. Betting bonuses are legal in Kenya when offered by BCLB-licensed operators. All sportsbooks listed on TrAfrikaAffiliates hold valid Kenyan licences issued by the Betting Control and Licensing Board.
